## About me

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the [University of Genoa](https://unige.it/en) in July 2026, at the [Perception and Interaction Lab](https://pilab.unige.it) of the [DIBRIS](https://dibris.unige.it/en) department. You can read my thesis entitled "_Designing Interactive XR Environments: Safety, Engagement, and Task Effectiveness Across Immersive and Non-Immersive Systems_" [here](https://unige.iris.cineca.it/handle/11567/1310177). 

With a background in bioengineering and a passion for immersive technologies, I focus on the design, development, and evaluation of XR applications. My research spans human–computer interaction, immersive visualization, and interactive technologies, with a particular interest in their applications for motor rehabilitation and education.

### My PhD work

I hold a Master's degree in Bioengineering (110/110 cum laude) and a PhD in Computer Science, specializing in eXtended Reality (XR) and Human–Computer Interaction. During my PhD at the Perception and Interaction Lab of the University of Genoa, I worked on technologies for motor rehabilitation, in particular a suite of exergames for upper-limb rehabilitation in children, controlled through wearable devices, within the [Fit4MedRob](https://www.fit4medrob.it/) project. My work included software development, hardware and software integration, design and execution of experimental studies, data analysis, project documentation management, and collaboration with clinicians and research centers. The system will soon be tested in a clinical trial with patients, approved by the Italian Ministry of Health, and has been the subject of several scientific publications.

## Research interests

My research focuses on:

- Design, development and evaluation of eXtended Reality (XR) applications
- Immersive visualization technologies
- Human–computer interaction and user-centered design
- Applications of XR in motor rehabilitation and education

## Community involvement

Beyond my research activities, I have been actively engaged in the XR community. I organized two editions of [XRehab](https://sites.google.com/view/xrehab/home), the workshop on XR for rehabilitation held within IEEE ISMAR: the [2025 edition](https://sites.google.com/view/xrehab/home) and the [2026 edition](https://sites.google.com/view/xrehab2026/home).

I was a member of the [DoCS group](https://docs-dibris.github.io), an association of PhD students in Computer Science that promotes seminars and other initiatives to foster collaboration and exchange of ideas among doctoral candidates, for the whole duration of my PhD. Within DoCS, I was the main organizer of the [Computer Science Workshop 2023](https://docs-dibris.github.io/docs/Computer%20Science%20Workshop/csw23.html) and [Computer Science Workshop 2024](https://docs-dibris.github.io/docs/Computer%20Science%20Workshop/csw24.html). The CSW is a unique event fully organized by PhD students, aiming to provide peers from different areas of computer science with a broader perspective on the discipline. Each year, the workshop hosts high-profile academic speakers from diverse subfields of computer science, both Italian and international, and includes contributions from local companies to showcase industry perspectives and research opportunities.

For three years I also served as PhD students' representative within the DIBRIS department, an experience that strengthened my organizational and coordination skills.
